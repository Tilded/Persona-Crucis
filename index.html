Take a prepared prompt to the AI service you prefer. Review the reading and choose a direction.

Build the character in manageable stages. Keep the useful material, revise the weak parts, and leave unknowns alone.

IV

Prepare

Review the GobboNet fields, correct anything necessary, and download a clean character JSON.
